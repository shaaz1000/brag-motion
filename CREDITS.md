Both upstream projects are worth using on their own: huashu-motion for motion graphics of any kind (with a Chinese-first community and docs), /brag for one-command launch videos on HyperFrames, or with no tooling at all as `/brag-slim`.

The example films in the README are of two apps by the same author, **Noor** (an iOS app for daily Muslim practice) and **Novic** (clinic software); every name and number in them is the apps' own demo data.
